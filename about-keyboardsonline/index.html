About KeyboardsOnline

About KeyboardsOnline — free online keyboards for 100+ languages, built to make typing in any language simple, fast and private.

KeyboardsOnline helps you type in different languages directly from your browser. Choose a language keyboard, type your text, copy it, and use it anywhere without installing extra software.

This GitHub Pages edition starts with 25 keyboards. The original, full site is at https://keyboardsonline.net/.

It is designed for students, writers, translators, language learners, social media users and anyone who needs quick multilingual typing.
